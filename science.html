🔬 Real NASA Science in Astro World

Astro World was built for students (ages 7–12), educators, and parents to teach the real-world physics, biological challenges, and strict resource constraints of deep-space survival. Every metric and gameplay event is anchored directly in authentic NASA open datasets.

📐 How the Game Math Works (Traceable Mechanics)

  • Space Weather to Shield Multiplier: In Game 1, an active X-class flare or solar proton event (SEP) doubles the required shield power ($2.0\times$ multiplier). An M-class flare raises it to $1.4\times$. If the outpost cannot supply the required power, shield integrity drops by 10% per unsupplied unit, eventually stressing crew health.
  • Radiation Attenuation Formula: In Game 2, incoming solar radiation dose ($D_{in}$) is reduced by layered shield materials. Each layer acts as an exponential attenuator: $D_{absorbed} = D_{in} \times \prod (Factor_i)$. Water walls attenuate protons strongly ($0.40$), Regolith absorbs moderately ($0.50$), and Active magnetic shielding attenuates at ($0.65$). No layout can achieve $0$ mSv because space radiation always has a background penetrating component (Galactic Cosmic Rays).
  • Life Support Power Priority: Keeping the habitat's life support above 3 daily power units maintains positive oxygen balance. Falling below 3 causes an oxygen deficit of $-8\%$ per unpowered unit per turn.

🔍 What We Simplified (Honest Transparency)

• Game-Scaled Lunar Night: A true lunar night lasts 14 Earth days. In our 10-turn mission, the lunar night lasts 3 turns so students can experience its challenge during a standard class period.

• Flare Class vs. Radiation Danger: In real space weather, flare X-ray classification does not always correlate 1:1 with particle radiation. Solar Energetic Particles (SEP) and CME shock geometry are the primary biological hazard. We simplify this into an intuitive threat level for young students while explicitly teaching the difference on screen.

• Active Magnetic Shielding: We clearly label magnetic field generators as an experimental concept currently being researched by NASA and academic scientists. It is not yet deployed on human spacecraft due to extreme mass and superconductor power constraints.

• Suit Procedures: Real EMU and xEMU contingency checklists involve hundreds of steps and ground control verification. Our suit triage game simplifies these into 3 key ordered actions to teach causal logic without overwhelming younger players.

🍎 Teacher & Parent Corner

Astro World is designed for direct classroom use, science clubs, and homeschool curriculum. Use these 3 guided inquiry questions with your students:

  1. The Water Paradox: Why is water both our most precious life-support drink AND one of the best shields against solar protons? How does hydrogen stop radiation?
  2. Autonomous Astronauts: If a radio message takes 20 minutes to reach Mars from Earth, why must astronauts be trained to fix life support emergencies without waiting for Mission Control?
  3. Energy Storage on the Moon: How would you survive 14 straight days of darkness and freezing cold on the Moon with zero sunlight for solar panels?
💡 Teacher Tip: Seeded Classroom Missions
Every game generates a 6-digit Mission Seed (displayed at the top of Game 1). You can give the exact same seed number to every student in your class so they all face the exact same sequence of solar storms and equipment challenges, enabling fair comparative discussion!
